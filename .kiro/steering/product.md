# Product
GopherGhost resurrects the Gopher protocol (RFC 1436) as a spooky CRT reader that converts modern web pages into plain-text Gopher menus.
Audience: people on slow connections, screen-reader users, and anyone tired of cluttered pages.
Tone: playful-haunted copy ("summoning...", "links beyond the veil"), but every interaction must stay keyboard- and screen-reader-accessible.
